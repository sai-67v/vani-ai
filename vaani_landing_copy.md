# ✦ Vaani AI: Brand Remix & Landing Copy ✦

## 1. Aesthetic Direction
- **Texture**: Raycast-like fine noise/grain overlaid on ultra-dark gradients.
- **Surfaces**: Premium glassmorphism panels (harsh lighting, subtle white/mint borders, dropping shadows, and `backdrop-blur`).
- **Accents**: Soft mint glow (e.g., `#A7F3D0` or tailored HSL) for active states, typography highlights, and waveform visualizers.
- **Hero Visual**: Real-time voice AI representations featuring a clean, responsive mint waveform paired with a scrolling, glass-paneled mock transcript reading in real-time.
